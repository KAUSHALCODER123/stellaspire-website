Who this is for

Why Singapore teams hire in India.

A Singapore head office rarely has the headcount budget for a full regional finance team, and local hiring is tight. India gives depth at a cost that works, provided somebody senior is doing the filtering.

  • Regional CFOs and finance directors a controller, a consolidation manager and an FP&A team in India reporting into Singapore.
  • Businesses weighing local versus offshore roles that genuinely belong offshore rather than being contested under the Fair Consideration Framework.
  • Fintech, trading and commodity firms product control, treasury operations and regulatory reporting talent with the volume to practise on.
  • Heads of data and engineering analytics and AI hires in Bengaluru or Hyderabad who can work a full Singapore day.

How we work

One recruiter, from brief to joining day.

You will not be handed to an account manager after the kick-off call.

The person who takes your brief runs the search, interviews the candidates and stays on the offer. Shortlists carry current pay in INR, notice period, the reason for the move and an honest note on the gaps. Where we think the brief is unwinnable at the approved band, we say so in week one.

Setting it up

Employment, payroll and the rules that matter.

Two sets of rules: Singapore's for passes, India's for employment.

Hiring into Singapore

Employment Pass and S Pass

If the seat is in Singapore, the pass framework and the Fair Consideration Framework advertisement sit with your HR team. We will tell you plainly when a role is unlikely to clear the qualifying salary and belongs in India instead.

Most common

India hiring, no entity needed

Early India hires go through an employer of record or our payroll. CPF has no Indian equivalent: the statutory items are provident fund and gratuity, and we draft offers that reflect them.

Scaling

Shared services build-out

For a full India shared service centre we hire in waves: leadership first, then process owners, then the analyst layer, against a plan agreed with Singapore finance.

Working hours

A full working afternoon in common.

Singapore is 2.5 hours ahead of India, so a 9am Singapore meeting is 6.30am in Bengaluru and a 6pm Singapore call is 3.30pm. Most teams settle on an 11am to 8pm IST day, which covers the whole Singapore afternoon. For month-end close this matters more than anything else in the brief, so we design the shift pattern before the search opens.
