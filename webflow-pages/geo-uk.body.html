Setting it up

Entity, payroll and the compliance questions.

UK finance teams ask the same questions, so here they are answered.

Before the entity

Employer of record

Early hires sit with an EOR while the Indian subsidiary is incorporated, with offers that move onto your payroll without renegotiation.

On your books

Indian statutory pay

Provident fund, gratuity, leave encashment and a 30 to 90 day notice period. These are permanent employees of an Indian company.

Where it applies

Contractors

An Indian contractor is tested under Indian employment law, not IR35, and we will say when a role is better as a permanent hire.

Working hours

Four and a half hours is the whole advantage.

India is 5.5 hours ahead of the UK in winter and 4.5 in summer. Most candidates we place work 12.30pm to 9.30pm IST, with real overlap from lunchtime onwards.
