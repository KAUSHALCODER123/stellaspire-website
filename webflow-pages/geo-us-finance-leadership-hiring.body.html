The short answer

Stellaspire works with US-headquartered companies. We run retained, confidential searches for CFOs, finance directors, controllers and FP&A heads, with a written market map before any shortlist.

Worth knowing

What a US parent needs from the first finance leader.

Two things that rarely sit in the same person: US GAAP close discipline, and the appetite to build an Indian team with no structure around them. We map both. Candidates out of US-headquartered GCCs are the obvious pool and they are expensive; candidates from Indian corporates cost less and need more support on reporting standards. We will tell you which trade-off your brief is actually making.

How we work

A market map before a shortlist.

Senior searches go wrong at the brief, not at the interview.

We start by writing down the realistic universe: the companies, the titles, what they pay and who has moved recently. You see that before any approach is made.
