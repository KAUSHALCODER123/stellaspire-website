Hiring from the UAE

Recruitment for UAE companies. Finance hires for Dubai and Abu Dhabi, and India back-office teams.

Half the finance market in Dubai is already Indian, which cuts both ways: the talent pool is familiar, and so is the competition for it. Stellaspire hires finance, accounting, analytics and AI people for UAE businesses, whether the seat is in Dubai, Abu Dhabi or a back office in Bengaluru.

The short answer

Stellaspire is a women-led search firm in Bengaluru. We hire finance, accounting, analytics and AI talent for UAE companies, source India-based candidates for relocation to Dubai and Abu Dhabi, and staff India back offices for Gulf groups. Same business week, ninety minutes apart.

Who this is for

Two kinds of UAE mandate.

Either you are hiring someone into the Emirates, usually from India, or you are putting a finance and analytics team in India to support the Gulf business. We run both, and the search looks quite different.

  • Family groups and holding companies a group financial controller or an FP&A head who has worked across several entities and jurisdictions.
  • DIFC and ADGM regulated firms finance, risk and compliance hires who understand the regulator and the reporting that comes with it.
  • Contracting, retail and logistics groups cost controllers, project accountants and consolidation specialists who can work with thin systems.
  • UAE businesses building an India back office an India shared service team for accounting, reporting and analytics, run on Gulf working hours.

How we work

Candidates who will actually move, at a number that works.

A Gulf offer is a family decision, and that is where most failed searches fail.

For relocation hires we test the real question early: schooling, spouse employment, the all-in package against what they keep in India today, and whether they have lived in the Gulf before. For India-based hires we benchmark against the GCCs in the same city. Either way, the shortlist carries each candidate's current package in writing.

Setting it up

Visas, Emiratisation and the package.

The compliance layer is yours, but we will not send you candidates who break it.

Mainland and free zone

Work permits and visas

Employment visas are sponsored by your entity through MOHRE, or by the free zone authority in DIFC, ADGM, DMCC and others. We confirm each candidate's current visa status and availability before shortlisting.

Where they apply

Emiratisation targets

Mainland companies above the skilled-worker threshold carry Emiratisation quotas. If a seat is earmarked for an Emirati national, say so at the brief and we will run that search separately rather than mixing the pools.

Every offer

Package, not salary

Basic, housing, schooling, flights, end-of-service gratuity, and whether the role is DIFC or mainland. We present offers the way candidates compare them: an all-in annual AED figure against their current net position.

Working hours

Same week, ninety minutes apart.

India is 1.5 hours ahead of the UAE and both run a Monday to Friday week, so an India team supporting a Dubai business is effectively inside the same working day. That is why Gulf groups have moved so much accounting and reporting work to Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Pune. For relocation hires it also means candidates can interview in person over a weekend.
