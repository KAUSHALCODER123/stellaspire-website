Who this is for

Two kinds of UAE mandate.

Either you are hiring someone into the Emirates, usually from India, or you are building a finance and analytics team in India to support the Gulf business. We run both.

  • Family groups and holding companies a group financial controller or FP&A head who has worked across several entities.
  • DIFC and ADGM regulated firms finance, risk and compliance hires who understand the regulator.
  • UAE businesses building an India back office a shared service team for accounting, reporting and analytics on Gulf working hours.
