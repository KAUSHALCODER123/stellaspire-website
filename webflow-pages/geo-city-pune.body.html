The Pune market

What the Pune market actually looks like.

Three things decide whether a Pune search closes.

  • Two markets in one city: the GCC belt hires like Bengaluru, slightly cheaper, and the manufacturing corridor pays on a different curve.
  • Retention is the real advantage. Pune candidates move less often than Bengaluru candidates at the same level.
  • Hinjawadi to Kharadi is a decision, not a detail, so we shortlist on where a candidate lives.
