The Pune market

What the Pune market actually looks like.

Three things decide whether a Pune search closes.

  • Two markets in one city. The GCC belt in Hinjawadi, Kharadi and Magarpatta hires like Bengaluru, slightly cheaper. The manufacturing and automotive corridor hires on plant finance, costing and engineering, and pays on a different curve entirely.
  • Retention is the real advantage. Pune candidates move less often than Bengaluru candidates at the same level, partly because the alternative employer is usually across town rather than across the road.
  • Commute splits the city. Hinjawadi to Kharadi is a decision, not a detail. We shortlist on where a candidate lives, which is also why Pune offers close more predictably.
