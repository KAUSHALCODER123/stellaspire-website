Who this is for

What US teams come to us for.

Most of our American clients are not opening a captive center on day one. They need three good people in India, quickly, and nobody in-house who can read an Indian resume. That is the gap we sit in.

  • US-headquartered companies hiring their first India team a controller, an FP&A lead or two data engineers, before there is an India HR function.
  • CFOs moving record-to-report or planning work offshore people who have run US GAAP close cycles and can talk to your auditors.
  • Series B to pre-IPO companies finance and data hires who have been through a US audit, a SOX readiness exercise or an S-1.
  • GCC leaders scaling a center the next layer of managers once the first twenty seats are full.

Working hours

The overlap problem, solved in the offer.

India is 9.5 to 12.5 hours ahead of the US mainland. Candidates who have worked with American teams know what that costs them, so we raise it in the first conversation rather than at offer stage. Most finance and analytics hires settle on a 2pm to 11pm IST day with a hard stop; engineering usually needs less overlap. We tell you which candidates will take a 7am Eastern stand-up and which will not.
