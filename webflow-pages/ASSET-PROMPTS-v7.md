# Image prompts for the v7 photo slots (8 October 2026)

Each slot sits in the left column of a long "process" section, next to the numbered steps.
Nothing shows on the page until the image is supplied, so you can add them one at a time.

**Format for every image:** landscape 4:3, at least 1600 × 1200 px, JPG or PNG. Send them back with the file name shown; I convert to WebP and upload.

**Shared style (paste at the end of every prompt):**
> Photorealistic editorial photograph, natural daylight from large windows, modern Bengaluru office with plants and warm wood, soft depth of field, candid not posed, nobody looking at the camera, muted palette with subtle lavender (#947AD3) and soft sky blue (#70B5F0) accents in clothing or decor, no logos, no text, no visible screens with readable content, Indian professionals, natural skin texture, shot on 35mm, 4:3.
