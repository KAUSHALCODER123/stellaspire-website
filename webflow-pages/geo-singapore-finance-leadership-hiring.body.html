How we work

A market map before a shortlist.

Senior searches go wrong at the brief, not at the interview.

We start by writing down the realistic universe: the companies, the titles, what they pay and who has moved recently. You see that before any approach is made. Then we run the search discreetly, interview everyone ourselves and present four or five people with written assessments against the brief.
