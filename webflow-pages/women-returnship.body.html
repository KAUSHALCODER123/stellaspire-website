Who it is for

A career break is not a skills gap.

Not sure your experience fits? Register anyway and we will talk it through.

  • Returning after a break, whatever the reason or length.
  • Experience in finance, analytics, data or technology, such as CAs, FP&A, audit, analysts and engineers.
  • Open to full-time, hybrid or flexible roles in Bengaluru or elsewhere in India.
