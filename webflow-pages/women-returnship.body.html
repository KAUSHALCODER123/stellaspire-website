Programme overview

A career break is not a skills gap.

Women step away from work for many reasons: caregiving, health, relocation or study. Returning can feel daunting when job ads ask for unbroken experience. Our programme exists to change that, for returners and for the employers who want to hire them.

The short answer

The Stellaspire women returnship programme is a career-restart pathway for experienced women professionals in Bengaluru and across India. We understand your experience and goals, match you with relevant roles at employers open to returners, and support you through interviews, offers and your return to work.

Who it is for

Experienced women, ready to return.

If you are not sure whether your experience fits, register anyway. We will talk it through with you.

  • Women professionals returning after a career break, whatever the reason and whatever the length of the break.
  • Prior experience in finance, accounting, analytics, data or technology, such as CAs, FP&A, audit, business analysts, data analysts and engineers.
  • Open to full-time, hybrid or flexible roles in Bengaluru or elsewhere in India, depending on the opportunity.
  • Ready to refresh and grow, with honest conversations about where you are today and where you want to go next.

Two sides of one programme

Matching returners with employers who want them.

A successful return depends on the right role, the right employer and an honest process.

We work with both sides: helping returners present their experience with confidence, and helping employers write roles, run interviews and set expectations that make returning realistic.
