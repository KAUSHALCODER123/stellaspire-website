The Hyderabad market

What the Hyderabad market actually looks like.

Three things decide whether a Hyderabad search closes.

  • Cost and retention are the draw: below Bengaluru on fixed pay, and people stay longer.
  • The employer set is concentrated in HITEC City, Gachibowli and the Financial District, which makes it easy to benchmark.
  • Senior supply is thinner, so director-level searches usually run local candidates alongside people returning home from Bengaluru, Chennai or Pune.
