Who this is for

Why Singapore teams hire in India.

Local hiring is tight and head-office budgets rarely stretch to a full regional finance team. India gives depth at a cost that works, provided someone senior does the filtering.

  • Regional CFOs and finance directors a controller, a consolidation manager and an FP&A team in India reporting into Singapore.
  • Fintech, trading and commodity firms product control, treasury operations and regulatory reporting talent.
  • Heads of data and engineering analytics and AI hires in Bengaluru or Hyderabad who can work a full Singapore day.

How we work

One recruiter, from brief to joining day.

You will not be handed to an account manager after the kick-off call.

The person who takes your brief runs the search, interviews the candidates and stays on the offer. If the brief is unwinnable at the approved band, we say so in week one.

Setting it up

Employment, payroll and the rules that matter.

Two sets of rules: Singapore's for passes, India's for employment.

Hiring into Singapore

Employment Pass and S Pass

The pass framework sits with your HR team, and we will say plainly when a role is unlikely to clear the qualifying salary.

Most common

India hiring, no entity needed

Early hires go through an employer of record or our payroll; the statutory items are provident fund and gratuity, not CPF.

Scaling

Shared services build-out

For a full India centre we hire in waves: leadership first, then process owners, then the analyst layer.

Working hours

A full working afternoon in common.

Singapore is 2.5 hours ahead of India. Most teams settle on an 11am to 8pm IST day, which covers the whole Singapore afternoon and month-end close.
