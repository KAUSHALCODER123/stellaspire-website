The Kolkata market

What the Kolkata market actually looks like.

Three things decide whether a Kolkata search closes.

  • The accounting base is deep and loyal, and candidates stay with an employer for years rather than months.
  • Shared services sit in Salt Lake Sector V and New Town, with pay benchmarked below Bengaluru and Hyderabad.
  • Senior searches often run local candidates alongside people returning home from Bengaluru, Mumbai or the Gulf.
