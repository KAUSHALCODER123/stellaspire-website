The Bengaluru market

What the Bengaluru market actually looks like.

Three things decide whether a Bengaluru search closes.

  • GCCs set the benchmark, which keeps pay high and tenure short.
  • The AI premium is real and still moving, so a band approved a year ago usually needs revisiting.
  • Commute is a live constraint, so we shortlist on location as well as skill.
