The Mumbai market

What the Mumbai market actually looks like.

Three things decide whether a Mumbai search closes.

  • Financial services set the price, and they pay in bonus as well as fixed. A brief benchmarked against a manufacturing band will not land here.
  • Geography decides acceptance. We ask where people live before we shortlist them.
  • Ninety-day notice periods and counter-offers are normal, so we stay close to candidates through the whole notice period.
