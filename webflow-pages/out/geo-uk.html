The short answer

Stellaspire is a women-led search firm in Bengaluru. We hire finance, accounting, analytics and AI talent in India for UK companies, work a 4.5 to 5.5 hour difference that suits both sides, and benchmark pay against the GCCs you are competing with.

Who this is for

Where UK clients usually need us.

The problem is rarely sourcing. It is that a London hiring manager cannot tell a strong Indian CV from a well-written one, and the agencies they have used send fifty of both.

  • UK groups setting up a shared service centre a finance manager and a controller first, then the team underneath them.
  • Finance directors consolidating group reporting people fluent in IFRS and UK GAAP who have closed books for a UK parent.
  • Scale-ups and PE-backed businesses a small senior India team rather than a large junior one.
  • Heads of data analytics and engineering hires who can hold their own with the UK business, not only build the pipeline.

How we work

Six CVs, each one worth reading.

We would rather send four strong profiles late than twenty on Friday.

Each CV arrives with a written note: current and expected pay in INR, notice period, what the candidate actually owns today and where they are weak. We interview everyone ourselves, and when a brief is priced below the market we say so rather than running a search we expect to lose.

Setting it up

Entity, payroll and the compliance questions.

UK finance teams ask the same four questions, so here they are answered.

Before the entity

Employer of record

Early hires often sit with an EOR while the Indian subsidiary is incorporated. Offers are drafted so that moving onto your own payroll is administrative rather than a renegotiation.

On your books

Indian statutory pay

Provident fund, gratuity, leave encashment and a standard 30 to 90 day notice period. None of this resembles an IR35 question: these are permanent employees of an Indian company.

Where it applies

Contractors

If you engage an Indian professional as a contractor, the test is Indian employment law, not IR35. We will tell you when a role is better off as a permanent hire.

Working hours

Four and a half hours is the whole advantage.

India is 5.5 hours ahead of the UK in winter and 4.5 in summer. A 10am London stand-up is mid-afternoon in Bengaluru, which is why UK and India teams work better together than UK and US ones. Most candidates we place work 12.30pm to 9.30pm IST with real overlap from lunchtime onwards. The pattern goes in the brief so nobody discovers it at offer stage.
