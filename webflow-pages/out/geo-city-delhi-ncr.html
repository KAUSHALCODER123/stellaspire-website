The Delhi NCR market

What the Delhi NCR market actually looks like.

Three things decide whether a Delhi NCR search closes.

  • Gurugram is the GCC and consulting belt. Cyber City, Golf Course Road and Udyog Vihar hold most of the finance, analytics and consulting talent, and pay is benchmarked against global parents rather than local ones.
  • Noida and Greater Noida are stronger on technology, services and shared service centres, usually at a lower cost base and with better retention. For process-heavy finance work it is often the better choice.
  • Promoter-led businesses in Delhi hire differently. The decision maker is frequently the owner, the process is fast, and the fit question is about working style rather than structure. We brief candidates honestly about that before an interview.
