How we work

Hire the supervisor before the team.

Offshore teams fail when the only senior person is in Australia.

We push most clients to hire a manager in India first and build underneath them. It costs more in month one and saves the whole experiment. Shortlists carry current pay in INR, notice period, the systems the candidate has actually used and a note on where they will need support.

Setting it up

Employment, payroll and the compliance questions.

Your India hires are employed under Indian law, not the Fair Work Act.

No India entity

Employer of record

Early hires sit on an EOR or our payroll. Offers are written so that moving onto your own Indian entity later changes nothing for the employee.

What replaces super

Indian statutory items

There is no superannuation in India. The statutory equivalents are provident fund and gratuity, and notice periods of 30 to 90 days are normal. Awards, penalty rates and Fair Work obligations do not apply to Indian employees.

Professional services

Data and client confidentiality

Accounting and advisory firms usually need client-data terms, access controls and a confidentiality undertaking per candidate. We raise it at the brief so it is not a late surprise.

Working hours

Mornings in Sydney, afternoons in Bengaluru.

India is 4.5 to 5.5 hours behind eastern Australia depending on daylight saving, and 2.5 to 3.5 behind Perth. A 9am Sydney stand-up is 3.30am IST, which does not work; a 2pm Sydney call is 8.30am IST, which does. Most teams run 8.30am to 5.30pm IST and keep the Australian afternoon for shared work.
