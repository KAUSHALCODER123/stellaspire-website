Setting it up

Employment, payroll and the compliance questions.

Your India hires are employed under Indian law, not the Fair Work Act.

No India entity

Employer of record

Early hires sit on an EOR or our payroll, with offers written so moving to your own entity later changes nothing for the employee.

What replaces super

Indian statutory items

Provident fund and gratuity replace superannuation, notice periods of 30 to 90 days are normal, and awards and penalty rates do not apply.

Professional services

Data and client confidentiality

Accounting firms usually need client-data terms and a confidentiality undertaking per candidate, which we raise at the brief.

Working hours

Mornings in Sydney, afternoons in Bengaluru.

India is 4.5 to 5.5 hours behind eastern Australia. A 2pm Sydney call is 8.30am IST, so most teams keep the Australian afternoon for shared work.
