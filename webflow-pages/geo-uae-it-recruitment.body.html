Worth knowing

What is different about technology hiring for the Gulf.

For seats inside the UAE the question is whether the candidate will move and at what all-in package. For India-based seats it is whether they can work with a thin local team around them. Government, banking and logistics clients usually add data-residency and access requirements, which narrows the pool and belongs in the brief from day one.

How we work

We read the build history, not only the CV.

Every engineering market has candidates who interview better than they build.
