Fees and terms

Agreed in writing, before we start.

  • How fees are set. By role, seniority, number of positions and engagement model.
  • When fees are payable. Milestones are set out in writing for your chosen model.
  • Replacement terms. If a placed candidate leaves within the agreed period, replacement applies.
  • Candidates never pay. We never charge candidates for any part of the process.

What we need from you

A great search is a partnership.

  • A role brief and time with the hiring manager for the first conversation.
  • The pay range, and any flexibility on location or start date.
  • A named contact who can share feedback quickly.
